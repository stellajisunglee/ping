Add Ping to your Home Screen

iPhones only allow notifications from apps on the Home Screen, and Ping's calls arrive as notifications.

  1. Tap the Share button in Safari
  2. Choose Add to Home Screen
  3. Open Ping from your Home Screen

In Safari you can call Ping, but Ping can't call you.

Calls are recorded and transcribed so the Ping team can review them and improve Ping. By joining, you agree to the Privacy Policy and Terms of Service, which are in Settings.

                Who Ping is for

                Ping is for adults, 18 or older, in the United States.

                Your voice and your calls

                During a call, your voice goes from your phone to an AI voice service so the AI can reply. Ping uses three: OpenAI, and on some calls Deepgram or ElevenLabs, which each have an OpenAI model write the replies. ElevenLabs is set to keep no audio or transcripts of your calls. Ping records each call, both your voice and the AI's, and keeps a written transcript of it. After the call, your phone uploads the recording and the transcript to Ping's server, so the Ping team can listen to them and use them to improve Ping's conversations.

                Who sees your recordings

                Only the Ping team. Ping doesn't sell or share your recordings, play them for anyone else, or use them to identify you by your voice or copy your voice. Four companies handle your calls for Ping: OpenAI, Deepgram and ElevenLabs process the call audio and transcripts while you talk, and Render hosts Ping's server, where the recordings are stored.

                Turning recording off

                On most accounts, you can turn off "Use my calls to improve Ping" in Manage Account. Calls after that aren't recorded, and calls recorded before are kept. On beta tester and admin accounts, recording is part of testing, so it stays on.

                How long Ping keeps them

                Recordings and transcripts are kept until you delete your account. On beta tester and admin accounts, everything kept about your calls (recordings, transcripts, ratings, comments and call details) stays until you delete your account or the Ping team deletes it.

                What else Ping stores

                The name you give Ping to call you, and how to say it. How to say it goes to the voice service on each call so Ping can greet you by name; the name as you typed it is sent to OpenAI only to suggest how to say it.

                Which invite code you joined with and when, how many calls you've made, your time zone, settings (including your Japanese level), plan, streak, notification details, voicemails, recent call times, your word and sentence stacks, your contacts, timing numbers from each call, such as how long Ping took to reply, and numbers about your Japanese on each call, such as how long you spoke, how many different words you used, how often you used English or asked for help, and how many of your saved words you used. Your phone works these out from the call; only the numbers are sent.

                Your speaking level

                After a recorded call, Ping sends its transcript to OpenAI to estimate your Japanese speaking level (JLPT N5 to N1). Each estimate is kept with your account, and once there are five, Ping shows you the level your speaking sounds like.

                Conversations

                On a paid plan, the text of your recent calls is also used to personalize future calls.

                Cancelling and deleting

                If you move to a smaller plan, words and sentences beyond its limit, and on the free plan your saved conversations, clear after 30 days. Deleting your account in Manage Account removes your account data right away, including your call recordings and transcripts.

                Feedback

                After a call you can rate it and leave a comment. Ratings and comments are stored with that call and your account, so the Ping team can read them alongside it. Anything you send through Feedback in Settings is stored without your account attached.
