Add Ping to your Home Screen

iPhones only allow notifications from apps on the Home Screen, and Ping's calls arrive as notifications.

  1. Tap the Share button in Safari
  2. Choose Add to Home Screen
  3. Open Ping from your Home Screen

An app that calls you once a day to practice speaking Japanese, just like a friend would.

Ping is an AI. During a call, your voice goes to OpenAI so Ping can reply. Calls aren't recorded.

            Your voice

            During a call, your voice goes directly from your phone to OpenAI so the AI can reply. Ping doesn't record audio.

            What Ping stores

            Which invite code you joined with and when, how many calls you've made and the minutes they used, your time zone, settings, plan, streak, notification details, voicemails, recent call times, your word and sentence stacks, and your contacts.

            Conversations

            On a paid plan, the text of your recent calls is stored to personalize future calls. On the free plan, conversation text isn't stored.

            Cancelling and deleting

            If you cancel, words, sentences and conversations saved during your subscription clear after 30 days. Deleting your account in Manage Account removes your account data right away.

            Feedback

            Anything you send through Feedback is stored without your account attached.
